Ariane Dialer

Call from the contact. See the words live. Get the next product to pitch.

A click-to-call softphone inside Odoo with a live transcript and a live sales coach that suggests products from your own catalogue, at your own prices.

This app is free. Calls go through your own Twilio account. Live calling and the AI coach need an Ask Ariane account.

What it does

Call from the contact

A Dialler button sits next to every phone field. One click opens a side panel for that customer: owner, pricelist, amount due, last orders, open quotes and to-dos.

Live transcript

The conversation is written out line by line while the call runs, and kept on the call record and the contact's Calls tab afterwards.

Live coach card

While the customer talks, a coach card suggests products from your own catalogue, priced by your own pricelists and price rules, with free stock and quantity breaks. Prices you mark as "do not quote" never show.

Badges that help the pitch

  • New seller - products that started selling recently.
  • Clearance - products a sales manager flagged to move, with an optional end date.
  • Selling in your state - top sellers to shops in the customer's delivery state.

Filler lines

Short, safe lines the rep can say while the coach is thinking. You set them in Settings.

Wrap-up

Pick an outcome, write a note, set a call-back date. Odoo logs the call, adds a note to the customer or lead, and creates the call-back activity. "Do not call" can add the number to Odoo's phone blacklist.

Calling from a list

The idle panel shows today's calls. Call next, skip, or save and call the next one. Nothing dials without a click.

Incoming calls

Calls to your Twilio number can be answered in the panel, with the same customer card.

AI rep test call (off by default)

For trying the coach without a customer: an AI voice agent places a test call to a number you choose, and you play the customer. Nobody has it after install; an admin turns it on per user with the AI rep demo calls group (Settings > Users, developer mode). It then shows as a switch in the panel and a button next to phone fields.

Who it is for

B2B sales teams that sell by phone from Odoo, on Odoo.sh or self-hosted. Reps see the customer and the right products in one place, and managers see every call.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18 (Community or Enterprise) with Sales, Inventory and CRM.
  • Your own phone account. Bring your own Twilio account and numbers. Ask Ariane does not sell phone lines or numbers; your calls are carried and billed by your phone provider.
  • An Ask Ariane account for live calling, the live transcript and the coach. It is a paid monthly service, billed by Ask Ariane, not through the Odoo Apps Store.
  • Without an Ask Ariane account the panel runs in preview mode: the rep dials on their usual phone and uses the panel for the customer, products, quotes and the call log.

What leaves your Odoo

Nothing is sent anywhere until an admin enters the dialler service URL, tenant id and signing key in Settings > Sales > Dialler. Until then the panel runs in preview mode and every lookup, search, quote and call log stays inside your Odoo.

Once connected, the browser of a user with the Dialler access right sends this to the Ask Ariane dialler service. This includes personal data about your customers: the customer's name and the phone number dialled.

  • Sign-in pass (when Odoo opens, refreshed while it stays open) - your database uuid, your tenant id, the Odoo user id and the user's company ids, signed with your key.
  • Each call placed - the number dialled (personal data), the contact's id and the Odoo call record's id.
  • Coach context for the call - sent when the live coach is on for the call, and on every AI rep test call. It holds: the phone number (personal data), the Odoo call id, the contact's (partner) id, the company id and the Odoo user id; the customer's name (personal data), delivery state, customer tier and pricelist name; up to 60 products that may be offered to that customer, each with name, internal reference, price and currency for that customer, free stock, availability text, category, brand, shipping note, badges, a "featured" flag and, for "selling in your state" products, their rank and the state they were ranked in; and the catalogue names the coach listens for (vendor, brand and category names only).
  • AI rep test call (only for users an admin gave the group) - the number to call, the contact's id, the Odoo call id and the coach context above, sent to the service's test-call route (/v1/dialer/demo-call); the service's AI voice agent then places the call.
  • Call audio - carried by your own Twilio account through the in-browser softphone (the Twilio Voice SDK, shipped inside the module). On calls the live coach is on for, and on AI rep test calls, the service transcribes the call to give the coach its words.
  • Twilio's own reporting - the Twilio Voice SDK, as Twilio ships it, reports call-quality data (connection and audio statistics) to Twilio's servers. This module keeps that default.

What comes back: the service posts each call's transcript and AI summary to this module's own signed route, and they are stored on the call record in your Odoo. Call recording is off unless it is switched on for your Ask Ariane account. When it is on, the service keeps the audio encrypted in its own store (365 days unless your account sets another period) and deletes the copy at Twilio; your Odoo receives only a recording reference id, never the audio. The panel measures call-health timings in the browser and stores them in your Odoo; it does not send them to the Ask Ariane service.

Not sent: product searches typed in the panel, quotes, wrap-up notes, outcomes and the rest of your database stay in Odoo. Answering an incoming call sends nothing extra from Odoo.

Set up in five steps

  1. Install the app.
  2. Give your reps the Dialler / User access right, and managers Dialler / Manager.
  3. Open an Ask Ariane account with the Connect to Ask Ariane button (here or in Settings > Sales > Dialler) and link your own Twilio account there.
  4. In Settings > Sales > Dialler, paste the service URL, tenant id and signing key from Ask Ariane.
  5. Open a contact and click the Dialler button next to the phone number. Logged calls are under Sales > Orders > Dialler calls.
