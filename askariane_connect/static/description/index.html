Ask Ariane OS

Connect your Odoo to Ask Ariane for a free business diagnostic.

An administrator ticks the companies Ask Ariane may read, signs in on askariane.ai and confirms inside Odoo. Then your Odoo sends a small daily snapshot of counts for those companies only. No names, no free text, no customer records.

This app is free. It works on Odoo.sh and self-hosted Odoo 18.

How it connects

  1. Open Ask Ariane > Connection (Settings administrators only), tick 1 to 10 companies and click Connect.
  2. Your Odoo makes a new key pair. The private key stays in your Odoo. Ask Ariane only ever receives the public key.
  3. You sign in on askariane.ai and approve. You come back to Odoo.
  4. Odoo shows the Ask Ariane account the data will go to, the companies and the fields. Nothing is stored or sent until the administrator who started the connection clicks Confirm.

Every field that leaves your Odoo

When you click Connect (in the address your browser opens on askariane.ai)

  • origin: your Odoo's web address (scheme and host, from web.base.url)
  • db_uuid: your database's id (database.uuid)
  • company: the name of your current company (as a label, up to 120 characters)
  • company_ids: the ids of the companies you ticked
  • state and challenge: one-time random values that secure the sign-in

When you come back from askariane.ai (server to server)

  • the one-time code, the PKCE verifier, the new public key and the origin

When you click Confirm

  • the enrolment id Ask Ariane gave in the step before, signed with your new key

Once a day: the snapshot (schema snapshot.v1)

  • schema: always "snapshot.v1"
  • odoo_version: for example "18.0"
  • modules: the technical names of installed modules (up to 400)
  • companies: for each approved company only (1 to 10):
    • odoo_company_id: the company's id (not its name)
    • customers: number of contacts of that company marked as customers
    • products: number of products of that company
    • users: number of active internal users with access to that company
    • open_quotations: number of quotations in draft or sent
    • open_invoices: number of posted customer invoices not fully paid
    • monthly_sales: for this month and the 11 before it, the month (YYYY-MM) and the untaxed total of posted customer invoices

Each count reads only records that belong to that company. Records shared between companies (with no company set) are not counted. Without the Sales or Invoicing apps, those counts are 0. No company names, contact names, emails, addresses, product names or any other text are sent.

Key upkeep

  • The key lasts 90 days. With fewer than 30 days left, the daily job sends a new public key and a proof made with it, signed with the current key.
  • Every signed request carries the key id, a key generation number, your pinned Odoo web address, a timestamp, a random nonce and the signature.

Your control

  • Disconnect from Ask Ariane > Connection. Odoo tells Ask Ariane, then deletes the key from your Odoo whatever the answer. You can also disconnect from your account on askariane.ai, without your Odoo.
  • Delete my data: the Ask Ariane account owner can delete the snapshots, the connection and the account's data from askariane.ai.
  • Staging copies go quiet: when Odoo.sh makes a staging or dev copy, the key and any pending connection are wiped (Odoo neutralize). If your Odoo's web address changes, nothing is sent until it is restored or you connect again.
  • Only Settings administrators can connect or disconnect, and only the administrator who started a connection can finish it.
